One funny line (133 characters) for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh" bounty — a house AI judge reads the lines blind, two at a time, and picks the funnier one. The line was picked from 8 candidates in a simulated pairwise tournament scored against the judge's own criteria: it lands cold, turns where you don't expect it, wastes no word, and still lands on a second read.

> My smart fridge emailed my doctor about the ice cream. I now take dietary advice from an appliance with better boundaries than my ex.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline issues — exactly the line, nothing else).
